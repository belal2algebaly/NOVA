# NOVA Competitor Research — 25-Phase Intelligence Upgrade

## Stream A — 10 phases for Direct Competitor Discovery
1. Local-market query priority
2. Country/TLD prequalification
3. Currency/location snippet qualification
4. Commercial-intent filtering
5. Multi-query candidate agreement
6. Marketplace/social/directory noise suppression
7. Candidate pre-score before crawling
8. Local → regional → global expansion ladder
9. Search-overlap based on repeated discovery evidence
10. Hard Direct Competitor Gate after full validation

## Stream B — 5 phases for Competitor Data Reading
11. Multi-page store crawl instead of homepage-only
12. Sitemap-assisted product/category discovery
13. JSON-LD Product extraction: name, price, currency, category, availability
14. Robust 10th–90th percentile price range + median
15. Assortment coverage: categories, product samples, product URL estimate, source pages

## Stream C — 10 phases for NOVA Logic Quality
16. Market is the highest-weight signal
17. Product similarity promoted above generic keyword similarity
18. Different known markets receive hard score caps
19. Price similarity becomes Unknown when currencies conflict
20. Missing evidence reduces confidence instead of becoming zero/pass
21. Crawl evidence quality calibrates confidence
22. Low-coverage scans cannot produce high-confidence Direct labels
23. Category + product minimum thresholds for Direct status
24. Needs Verification state for evidence-poor candidates
25. Provenance/freshness fields: pages scanned, extraction quality, observed timestamp

## Core rule
Same industry is never sufficient for Direct Competitor status. Direct requires strong local-market evidence, meaningful assortment overlap, and adequate evidence confidence.
