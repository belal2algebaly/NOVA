## Buyer Persona evidence contract
NOVA may accept orders, reviews, sales by city/region, demographics when explicitly supplied, product-level sales, AOV/repeat data, discounts, returns/refunds, support/WhatsApp exports, surveys, Meta/Google/GA4/CRM exports and other customer materials.

NOVA must never infer a demographic or psychological fact just because a segment resembles a stereotype. Facts are observed, inference is labeled, and missing evidence is Unknown.
