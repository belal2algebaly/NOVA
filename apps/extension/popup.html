How NOVA scans

NOVA uses evidence-first checks on the rendered page. It waits for dynamic content, performs a controlled page sweep for lazy-loaded sections, detects fixed/sticky purchase actions, and only passes a check when it can point to supporting evidence. Missing evidence never becomes a silent pass.

The CRO layer prioritizes findings that affect the buying decision: purchase action reachability, delivery/returns clarity, product information, social proof, visual hierarchy, contrast, friction, mobile usability and trust. Page-type requirement models are different for PDP, PLP, Cart, Checkout and Homepage. Subjective judgments remain marked for human review.
